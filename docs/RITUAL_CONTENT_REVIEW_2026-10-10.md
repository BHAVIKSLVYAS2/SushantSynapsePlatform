# Ritual preparation content review

Reviewed 10 October 2026. All 15 English/Hindi guides now include reviewed material notes and officiant questions. These are original preparation summaries, not copied liturgical text, certified ritual instructions or universal family requirements.

| Guides | Reviewed references and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Daily puja | BAPS practices: devotional setup, explicitly tradition-specific |
| Ganesh | Drik Panchang Ganesh/Chaturthi preparation: durva, modak, optional betel/supari; counts left to officiant |
| Lakshmi/Diwali | Drik Panchang material list: separate rice/akshata, optional coins; no compulsory rare herbs |
| Satyanarayan | Hindu Temple of Tallahassee: panchamrit ingredients, family prasad recipe, rice/betel/cloth |
| Griha pravesh | Hindu Temple of Frisco: toran/mango leaves, conditional grains/milk custom |
| Namkaran | Hindu Temple of SiouxLand: conditional rice writing and gathering prasad; no infant feeding instructions |
| Vivah | SiouxLand wedding list: optional Laja and family clothing/symbols |
| Havan | Charleston Havan PDF, SiouxLand and Frisco: 15 entries grouped into setup, confirmed offerings and accompanying puja; no universal mixture, weights or offering count |
| Antyeshti, Prarthana sabha, Asthi | Himalayan Academy and AMSUK cultural references: adult funeral coordination, dignity, conditional male/female clothing and collection arrangements; UK legal procedures excluded |
| Shraddha | Drik Panchang and SiouxLand: conditional rice/pinda, sesame/kusha and officiant-approved substitutions |
| Days 10, 11, 12 | Hindu Janajagruti post-cremation reference: optional regional rites, variable Sapindikarana timing; mourning day counts distinguished from lunar tithi names |

Exact source URLs are attached to each guide in `frontend/guides.mjs`, `mourning.mjs` and `materials-review.mjs`. The former Nebraska homam URL redirected to a JavaScript shell and was replaced with [Charleston's accessible PDF](https://hindutemplechs.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/PujaSamaghri_Havan.pdf).

Foreign prices, large-event quantities, unsafe ingestion instructions and restrictive gender rules were excluded. Actual fire use needs officiant and venue approval. Medical/legal release and crematorium requirements take priority over general notes. Sources describe particular traditions; no endorsement, rights clearance or priest certification is claimed.

Astronomical accuracy is separate: the existing approximate engine and selected fixture verification remain unchanged. City-centre coordinates and regional defaults are provisional; ambiguous boundaries/months and family-specific dates still require confirmation.
