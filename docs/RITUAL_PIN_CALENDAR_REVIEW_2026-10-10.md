## Rules and accuracy limits

- Death tithi comes from geocentric Moon–Sun separation in 12-degree intervals. Masik and Barsi search lunar months/tithi intervals; dates never use fixed 30-day months or Gregorian year increments. Shraddha candidates use maximum local Aparahna overlap. Existing boundary/tie/no-overlap guards withhold dates.
- Chautha and days 10–13 are inclusive mourning-day counts, not lunar Dashami/Ekadashi/Dwadashi. Family sunrise-counting remains an explicit override.
- First Pitru Paksha after first Barsi is the existing selected policy, explicitly labelled; it is not a universal claim for every Hindu tradition. Special death tithis, Adhik/Kshaya Maas and family-specific exceptions require an officiant's confirmation. Amanta/Purnimanta state defaults are conveniences and can be overridden.
- Astronomy Engine and the Chitra/Spica sidereal month approximation are not a certified regional Panchang or exact Lahiri ephemeris. Postal data cannot guarantee exact-address sunrise/Aparahna. Passing selected fixtures does not certify all ten future years or all traditions.
