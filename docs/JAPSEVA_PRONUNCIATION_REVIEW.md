sign-off. No teacher has reviewed or endorsed the learning library or recordings.

Vedic accents, initiation and tradition-specific recitation are not taught here.

## Source checks and their limits

- [Library of Congress Sanskrit romanization table](https://www.loc.gov/catdir/cpso/romanization/sanskrit.pdf)
  distinguishes long vowels, retroflex ṇ, ś and visarga/anusvāra. This supports
  explaining the letters, not certifying a mantra recording. The library retains
  its established IAST-style dot-above ṁ spelling; transliteration conventions
  may use dot-below ṃ for anusvāra.
- [Learn Sanskrit: vowels](https://www.learnsanskrit.org/guide/sounds/vowels/)
  describes vowel length and explicitly puts Vedic accent teaching outside its
  grammar guide. The app's new sound key follows the basic vowel distinction.
- [Learn Sanskrit: consonants](https://www.learnsanskrit.org/guide/sounds/consonants/)
  distinguishes aspiration and consonant places. Its nasal examples demonstrate
  why a single English “m” approximation cannot prescribe every context.

These are original educational/editorial sources. We have not copied their
lessons or audio. New guide text is an original short explanation, with links.
No new mantra text, ritual entitlement or doctrinal meaning is inferred from
these sources. Existing traditional source labels are retained.

## Questions for a qualified Sanskrit / chanting teacher

1. Check each Devanagari text and its transliteration character by character.
2. Check long vowels, aspirated bh/dh, ś/ṣ/s and dental/retroflex distinctions.
3. Check anusvāra and visarga in context, especially gaṁ, namaḥ and bhuvaḥ/svaḥ.
4. Listen to every complete recording. Confirm exact text, omissions, variants,
   pronunciation, intelligibility and suitability as a learning reference.
5. For Gayatri, identify the recitation tradition and assess whether accent,
   syllable length or verse/prelude variation needs explicit separate teaching.
6. Review each approximate English syllable aid; propose corrections without
   treating it as a replacement for the Sanskrit or a teacher's instruction.
7. Record name, qualifications, tradition, date, reviewed asset SHA-1, approved
   version, changes requested and explicit scope/permission for public credit.

Do not change any “pending” status to reviewed without that evidence. A freely
licensed recording and a successful browser playback test are separate from
pronunciation approval. No outreach or public teacher credit has been performed.
