# Everyday Rashifal reading

25 October 2026, gochar-7.

Reviewed the public horoscope experiences of
[AstroSage](https://www.astrosage.com/horoscope/),
[AstroSage Aries](https://www.astrosage.com/horoscope/aries.asp) and
[Astroyogi](https://www.astroyogi.com/horoscopes/daily/aries-free-horoscope.aspx).
They put readable everyday content and recognisable life areas near the start.
AstroSage explicitly describes its Indian Rashifal as Moon-sign based. Astroyogi
also exposes numerical positivity, lucky items and a consultation journey.
We adopt the readable content hierarchy, not copied predictions, numerical
outcome ratings, lucky items or sales prompts.

The previous primary card mostly described favourable/obstructed classifications
and timing. That is useful evidence but weak everyday reading. The revised card
uses the actual sampled Moon houses to select original editorial themes: self,
family/resources, communication, home, creativity, routine, cooperation, shared
responsibilities, learning, work, community and rest. It adds practical work/study,
spending and relationship suggestions. These associations are project editorial
choices using traditional house vocabulary, not independently accepted classical
life-event rules. They do not derive validated personal outcomes from coordinates.

One house produces one theme; two produce both themes and an explicit changing
focus message. More than two produces a broad-period message without reducing
the week to one daily theme. Uncertain Moon classifications or missing houses
withhold specific themes and life-area cards. The old computed classification
sets the pace suggestion; an outside-favourable house is not a prediction of bad
events. The result retains theme model, houses, mode, existing Moon counts and
timing trace. Identical factors can still share guidance. No random diversity,
invented luck scores or unsupported health/wealth promises are added.

Technical summaries, all nine grahas, timing, samples and source evidence remain
inside one expandable calculation section. The single shared platform header
still owns language and theme. Birth data, privacy and numerical engines are
unchanged. More readable text cannot guarantee that a general Moon-sign reading
matches an individual's circumstances; personal birth-chart interpretation and
independent astronomical, traditional and language acceptance remain separate.

The user's supplied Moon Signs sheet provides all twelve light and dark
illustrations. Twenty-four 190x206 WebP crops total 214,090 bytes; UI names remain
real translated text, with accessible button names and an explicit selected state.
Decorative images have empty alt text. Both sets follow the resolved shared theme,
including system theme. Assets are explicitly public-allowlisted. No new image
generation, icon font, third-party image requests or additional theme controller.

Verification is recorded in the functionality ledger after checks complete.
