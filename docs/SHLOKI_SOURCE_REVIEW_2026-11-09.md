## Editorial decisions

The collection admits 19 readings from the PDF's 21 topics. Sanskrit is checked
against the specific editions linked below, with whitespace, danda punctuation
and ordinary visarga presentation normalized. Existing textual variants are not
represented as a single universally certified version. Meanings are newly written,
short English/Hindi explanations; no modern publisher translations or audio are
copied. Vedic accent marks are not supplied. This is a text/source comparison,
not independent Sanskrit-scholar or recitation certification.

The two four-verse groups contain all four verses, rather than just the opening
verse printed in the PDF. Excerpts and traditional summaries are named accurately.
No claims that reading guarantees health, wealth, salvation or replacement of
complete ceremonies are introduced.

## Interface and boundaries

Prep has one collapsed Shloki collection above the ritual chooser. Its reader
loads only on opening or a `?shloka=<id>` deep link. A native selector and
Previous/Next controls show one reading at a time; four-verse groups stay together.
Larger type, optional meaning/source disclosures and separate UTF-8 text download
and copy controls are included. Sanskrit is marked `lang=sa` and protected from
generic interface translation. Sources open only on explicit user activation.

No new account, API, database, audio/provider request, localStorage key or document
upload is introduced. Checklist state and existing Vrat/Shraddha calculations
remain independent. Browser history and the existing retained Prep root carry
reading selection during navigation. Existing checklist PDF export excludes the
Shloki disclosure. No dedicated Shloki PDF export is claimed.
