# Copyright and legal risk review — 10 October 2026

This is a technical risk review and implementation proposal, not a legal opinion, clearance certificate or finding that infringement has occurred. Indian counsel must assess the operator, contracts, relevant exemptions and enforceability. No production settings, accounts, records, documents or publisher data were changed. Application fixes and deployment are not part of this audit.

The highest-priority findings are publisher/data-provider rights and the absence of public privacy/operator policies. Drik Panchang referencing is not the largest identified exposure.

## Scope and evidence

Inventoried all 108 distinct allowlisted frontend source files across 15 app owners, including aliases, shared UI, local raster/SVG assets and vendored bundles. Reviewed app documentation, composition/authentication, provider extraction/caching/publication, exports, family privacy/consent/photo removal, sponsorship and support controls. Source searches and targeted reads are not a forensic comparison of every line against every possible copyrighted work.

Read-only production checks examined 20 distinct HTML pages: showcase, support, Ritual Assist and its calendar, Moment Studio and certificates, Pocket Pause, DIGITAL SAMAJ, Tournament Lite, BatchFee Lite, home, Chambers, Fund Lens, News, Timetable Lite, Take a Break, sponsorship, Daily Spark, Decision Wheel and Team Mixer. All returned 200. `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/legal` and `/contact` returned 404. None of these 24 responses contained a privacy/terms/legal link. CSP was present; HSTS was absent. Anonymous page availability does not mean private API access was granted. Private content, administrator settings and actual business records were not accessed.

Evidence: `.publish/legal-audit-probes.json`; probe source `.publish/legal-audit-probes.cjs`. Current HTML probes did not detect analytics injection. Previous deployment evidence records Cloudflare Insights requests; settings/browser behavior must be checked before making a site-wide no-analytics claim. This review does not establish a breach, assess every deployment secret/host ACL, conduct a penetration test, clear trademarks or inspect licensing contracts held outside the repository.

### C1: News rights — first action

Source: `apps/news/frontend/engine.js` (`parseArticle`, `parseFeed`, `draftEdition`), `apps/news/backend/publication.js` and `repository.js`; the Cloudflare independent route reuses the extraction logic. The app truncates descriptions to 25 words and labels them excerpts. There is **no automatic legal “25-word safe limit.”** Crediting a publisher, linking to it, limiting words or generating accompanying jokes does not independently authorize extraction, storage or republication.

[Indian Express terms](https://indianexpress.com/terms-and-conditions/) prohibit automated extraction and systematic storage and require permission for content exploitation. Their [RSS policy](https://indianexpress.com/rss/) also restricts consumption to personal/non-commercial use. [BBC's published terms, section 15](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/usingthebbc/bbc_terms_of_use_19September2022english.pdf) distinguish permitted feed use from business metadata/RSS licensing. The BBC PDF is a published 2022 document; the latest applicable licence/terms must be confirmed before continued business use. These are documented terms conflicts/permission gaps, not a court finding that every headline or factual date is copyrighted.

Proposed technical fix: create a source-permission register with source URL, licence evidence, allowed fields, commercial use, cache period, transformations, exports and attribution. Default undocumented providers to unavailable in **both** Node and Worker. Apply the same permission checks to saved-edition public read, print/download and origin-offline rendering, not only new fetching. Preserve existing database editions; quarantine/restrict public access rather than destroying evidence or migrating user data. Remove the unsupported “reusable excerpts” assumption in validation copy.

An alternative is a smaller original news briefing using sources that expressly allow reuse. [PIB's copyright policy](https://www.pib.gov.in/content/3604_2_CopyrightPolicy.aspx?lang=1&reg=3) permits accurate acknowledged reproduction of its own material, excludes third-party material and restricts misleading/derogatory contexts. That is a scoped option, not permission for all government or publisher content. Do not attach satire in a way that misrepresents a government statement. If enough licensed dated stories are unavailable, publish fewer or show a clear unavailable state rather than fabricating ten stories.

### C2: Fund Lens source rights

Evidence: `apps/fund-overlap/backend/national-provider.js`, `provider.js`, `public-api.js`, `nav-provider.js`, ingestion sources and `infrastructure/cloudflare/fund-sources.mjs`. The reference recovery/cache/export design retains provider-derived data beyond a single personal page view.

[Tickertape terms](https://www.tickertape.in/meta/terms) restrict copying/exploitation and allow specified personal/non-commercial downloading; [Groww terms](https://groww.in/pages/terms-and-conditions-groww) prohibit copying/distributing/exploiting platform information/software. Public endpoint availability, lack of API keys and an ISIN/weight accuracy check are not redistribution permissions. No evidence reviewed establishes a negotiated exception. Factual underlying holdings and copyright in a curated presentation/database, as well as contract/access restrictions, require separate assessment.

A permitted integration should explicitly address caching, source attribution, normalized holdings, public JSON, browser downloads, commercial use and rate limits. Inspect MFapi at the actual `mfapi.in` domain; similarly named `mfapis.in` is a different service and its terms cannot clear this integration. Review each official AMC disclosure source and any upstream third-party components. Do not automatically substitute another undocumented scraper.

### P1–P3: Privacy and consent, including legal timing

The site needs app-specific explanations inside one coherent policy: Chambers clients/documents/hearings/payments, community family/photos/chat, academy students/fee records, tournament rosters and intentionally published token links, account sessions, device-saved timetables/organizations/preferences, and transient public-tool inputs. Show what enters SQLite, what stays on the device, what is sent to third-party sources, and who can access full backups. A “private” app field is not encryption against database administrators.

India's DPDP framework has staggered commencement. The [official November 2025 commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) brings principal processing/rights duties into force eighteen months after publication, rather than making every duty effective immediately in October 2026. Plan for the May 2027 phase and have counsel confirm precise applicability and deadlines. This review does not declare a present DPDP violation solely because a future duty lacks implementation. Existing confidentiality, contractual and applicable IT/privacy duties still need assessment; the phased schedule is not permission to collect unlimited family or health information.

Implement documented requests for access/correction/withdrawal/erasure with identity and authorization checks. Explain legitimate retention exceptions and legal holds. A family registrant's generic assertion is insufficient evidence of every adult's or minor's authority in every situation. Distinguish voluntary guardianship consent from relationship labels. Keep optional blood group, birth details and photos minimized and protected. Backups need a disclosed, bounded lifecycle; do not promise immediate erasure from every backup while retaining indefinite copies.

Publish a real privacy/contact/grievance channel chosen by the operator. Include Cloudflare hosting/logging and verify whether Web Analytics is enabled. [Cloudflare describes its analytics as not using cookies/localStorage for usage metrics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/); that alone does not mean no requests, no hosting metadata, or no disclosure obligation. Do not add a decorative consent banner that fails to control actual scripts, or claim a universal banner requirement without identifying audience and services.

### Commercial and regulated-claim boundaries

[SEBI investor guidance](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/cautiontoinvestor.html) explains investment-adviser registration and the distinction from widely available public information. Fund Lens' factual overlap tools and existing disclaimer are useful, but new recommendations or fee/referral models need a fresh IA/RA classification review.

[MIB's Digital Media division](https://www.mib.gov.in/en/node/1356?page=0) describes news/current-affairs publisher rules and grievance structures. News status must be reviewed against the applicable text, amendments and court orders rather than assuming every SaaS page is a news publisher or intermediary. Samaj messaging similarly needs an applicability assessment; existing message reporting does not substitute for a public legal complaint process.

[Consumer Affairs guidance](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1832906&lang=2&reg=48) addresses misleading advertisements. Keep no-guarantee language and paid-placement labels; do not promise audience/revenue, certificate accreditation, clinical outcomes, court synchronization or certified Panchang accuracy without evidence. Support/certificate donation wording must not imply a tax receipt. [Income Tax Department guidance](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/sites/default/files/2026-03/FAQs_80G%20Section%20mentioned%20%28final%29%20to%20upload.pdf) limits 80G deductions to eligible funds/institutions and prescribed conditions. Accounting advice is needed for actual receipts; foreign-contribution rules depend on recipient/purpose and are not assumed to apply to every ordinary business payment.

For confidential data operations, assess the scope of [CERT-In directions and FAQs](https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp), incident reporting and relevant log obligations. Do not confuse required security logs with an instruction to indefinitely retain every user photo/message. Public legal-services marketing, if later added, needs a separate professional-conduct review; the current Chambers workspace is not evidence of prohibited advocate solicitation.

## Safeguards already found

- Astronomy Engine retains its MIT notice in the shipped bundle; provenance/version/integrity are documented. QR bundle retains qrcode/dijkstrajs notices. These are not an identified missing-notice infringement.
- News art has image-generation prompts/provenance and fictional labels. No publisher photographs are used by the reviewed News renderer. That does not certify exclusivity or clear every possible visual resemblance.
- Cards, certificates, games and decorative scenes predominantly use original code/native canvas/CSS and system fonts; no external font or stock-image licence gap was identified in the allowlisted assets. This is not exhaustive plagiarism clearance.
- Ritual input stays in tab memory; unknown tithi and specified ambiguous dates are withheld. Accuracy scope and regional exceptions are visible. Reference links do not assert Drik endorsement.
- Certificates already say they are digitally generated and not independently verified. Issuer responsibility is stated.
- Family privacy projection, authenticated file access, role checks, consent records, secure-session-cookie logic and origin/CSP controls exist. They are safeguards, not legal certification.
- Sponsors are reviewed, clearly labelled paid placements and excluded from private workspaces. Payment recipient and campaigns remain unconfigured in documented release status; no transaction was performed in this audit.
- Showcase scenes are labelled illustrative/sample workflows; clinical, paid-entitlement and external-provider connections are not claimed as operational.

## Recommended implementation order and acceptance criteria

1. **Source-rights containment:** license-gate News and Fund providers in every deployment path; obtain licences or approved substitutes. Existing stored records remain preserved, while unlicensed public publication/export is restricted. No hidden scrape fallback may bypass the gate.
2. **Legal foundation:** add `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/contact`, `/legal/third-party-notices` and a rights/complaint procedure. All routes return 200 in Node and Cloudflare without origin dependence, have visible footer/sign-in links, operator-verified identity/contact, clear effective dates and app-accurate storage/processor explanations. Never copy another business's policies as if they describe this platform.
3. **Private-data workflows:** consent/guardian/authority records, requests and legal holds; documented blob/draft/audit/backup retention; verify API role enforcement and preserve required records. Tests use temporary DATA_DIR only.
4. **Ownership and claims:** a versioned asset/source register, trademark clearance, adapted-source permission, user-upload terms, independent/Panchang/financial/certificate scope and generic ad placeholders. Do not rename or remove brands simply because trademark clearance is pending.
5. **Operational/commercial readiness:** backup encryption/recovery, incident procedures, verified payment recipient/contact/refund handling, accounting advice, News/intermediary/financial classification and final Indian IP/privacy counsel review before expanded monetization.

Owner inputs needed for implementation: actual legal operator/contact, asset/adapted-code ownership evidence, any existing publisher/data licences, intended paid services, target audiences/minor handling and approved retention obligations. The audit can identify these gaps without fabricating those facts or contacting anyone on the owner's behalf.
