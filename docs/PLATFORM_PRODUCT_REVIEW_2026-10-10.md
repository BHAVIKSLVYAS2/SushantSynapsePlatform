# Platform product review — 10 October 2026

Review scope: app registry, README, architecture, latest functionality ledger, monetization/deployment documentation and current implemented calendar. This is a product review, not a fresh comprehensive security/browser audit. Public-page requests could not connect from the review session; that alone does not establish an outage. No app, account, payment, data or hosting changes were made.

Prioritize better connections between existing apps over another broad catalogue expansion:

1. **Ritual calendar reminders and preparation pack:** export individually calculated dates to an ICS calendar, preserving withheld dates and candidate warnings; downloadable bilingual preparation booklet, ceremony-specific material list, family-task assignment and an explicit WhatsApp share draft. Save private family calendars only with opt-in account storage and permissions. Human-reviewed regional Panchang rules are a prerequisite for stronger accuracy claims. Google Calendar supports individual events/reminders; never represent lunar anniversaries as fixed Gregorian annual recurrence. Reference: https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/concepts/events-calendars.
2. **Moment Studio organisation pack:** bulk certificates from CSV, consistent branding, signature/photo placement, saved templates and clearly defined premium exports. Separate saved organisations and permissions before serving independent customers. Keep useful free exports.
3. **Workspace offers:** publish honest feature comparisons, pricing proposed for validation, sample walkthroughs, WhatsApp enquiry and onboarding for BatchFee Lite, Tournament Lite and Chambers. No invented customers, revenue or testimonials. Verified checkout and server-enforced entitlements are not implemented; enquiry is not purchase confirmation.
4. **Return visits:** optional install-to-home-screen guidance, app favourites/recent activity, draft resume and scoped reminders. Some authenticated favourites/recents and timetable local saving already exist; extend these coherently rather than duplicating them. Sensitive saved records require API validation/access enforcement and SQLite, not browser-only persistence.
5. **Connected workflows:** Tournament results → Moment Studio winner certificates; Ritual Assist → ceremony/remembrance invitations and checklists; Timetable Lite → BatchFee academy onboarding. Share only explicitly selected fields, not whole private records.
6. **Growth and feedback:** original bilingual use-case landing pages, concise walkthroughs, accessible feedback/report-a-problem form and privacy-aware aggregate usage measures. Use observed metrics before claiming audience reach or setting sponsorship rates. Current WhatsApp enquiry remains a manual send flow.

Commercial prerequisites: always-on backend with recovery/backups; isolated organisation/customer workspaces (current Chambers is one shared workspace); server-authoritative payment verification/entitlements; complete operator/support and provider-rights decisions. Donations remain unconfigured according to recorded status; do not treat contact-number configuration as UPI setup. Automated WhatsApp delivery remains a separate provider/consent integration, not a feature of click-to-chat links.

Suggested sequence: calendar export/preparation pack → bulk certificate workflow → paid-app landing pages/onboarding → hosting/customer isolation/payment foundation → subscriptions. This is a prioritization proposal, not authorization to introduce paid services or external messages.
