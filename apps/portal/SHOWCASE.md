# Promotional demo player

Open `/showcase` to watch thirteen looping scenes. Featured apps lead in this order: **Ritual Assist, Moment Studio, Ritvayam**, followed by **Language change**. Then come Team Mixer, Timetable Lite, Take a Break, Decision Wheel, Chambers, DIGITAL SAMAJ, Tournament Lite, BatchFee Lite and the platform invitation. Workspace scenes link to sign-in and state their access requirements; paid checkout and published pricing are not configured, so no price or subscription availability is advertised. Every workflow is labelled **Demonstration · Sample data**. These are illustrative native HTML/CSS presentations rather than recordings or live app actions. The demo creates no account, booking, transaction or lead. There are no invented usage statistics, earnings or promised sponsorship results.

Featured scenes have automatic and clickable language previews: English/Hindi for Ritual Assist and Moment Studio; English/Hindi/Gujarati for Ritvayam and the dedicated language scene. Choosing a sample language pauses playback and reveals the completed example. Play resumes automatic cycling; choosing another scene clears the sample selection. The examples do not write the visitor's real language preference. Use Language in the shared header to change that preference. Content support varies by app: Ritual preparation guides and Studio artwork remain English/Hindi; the platform interface and Ritvayam reading feature offer all three languages.

Use Play/Pause, Previous/Next, Restart or a named scene. Scene length can be 8, 12 or 20 seconds. Space toggles playback, left/right arrows change scenes and F requests full screen when focus is outside ordinary controls. Full screen has its own pause/next/exit buttons. Hidden tabs pause elapsed time, and focused scene links pause playback so a visitor can follow them. Reduced-motion preference starts paused on a completed illustrative scene with transitions disabled.

`/showcase?screen=1` removes the introduction and partnership sections for a larger display. Keep a browser open on this URL to loop through the presentation; Exit display restores the page. Full screen requires a user gesture and browser support. The presentation contains no sound, stream or video provider. It is an HTML player, not a video export or a remote-managed signage service.

HTML player download has been removed. Use the hosted page or display mode; initial assets require internet.

Ownership is portal frontend only. Both page routes bypass Cloudflare origin checks; the Node static allowlist serves local development. Tests in `apps/portal/tests/showcase/browser.spec.js` use a temporary DATA_DIR and cover all thirteen scenes, featured order, automatic/manual language previews, unchanged visitor language preference, playback/pause, timings, keyboard navigation, fullscreen, display mode, light/dark/responsive layouts, reduced motion, no HTML export and no-JavaScript guidance. Source implementation uses fixed sample strings and no visitor-authored HTML.

Sponsorship forms open combined WhatsApp drafts; enquiry downloads are unavailable.
