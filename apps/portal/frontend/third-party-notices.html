GeoNames Indian postal locations

Ritual Assist uses a derived offline snapshot of GeoNames Indian postal coordinates, retrieved 10 October 2026, under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Multiple places are combined into median coordinates per PIN; inconsistent entries are rejected. The source supplies approximate coordinates without an accuracy warranty. No death details are sent to GeoNames.
