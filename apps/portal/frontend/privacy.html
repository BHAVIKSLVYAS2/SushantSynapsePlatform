SUSHANT SYNAPSE / INFORMATION

Privacy & data use

This notice explains how Sushant Synapse tools handle information. Updated 23 October 2026. It describes the current implementation; it does not promise anonymity, certified security or automatic deletion.

WhatsApp contact is available through our contact page. Public legal operator details are still being finalized. For a managed workspace, contact the person who provided your account. See contact and requests.

Beta notice preference

The beta acknowledgement stores only the current notice version under synapse-beta-acknowledgement in local storage on this browser, with session storage as a fallback. It is not linked to an account, sent to the server or kept as a legal consent audit. Review or forget it from the beta notice controls. If storage is blocked or cleared, the notice may reappear. The checkbox does not grant new tracking or data-sharing permissions. WhatsApp opens only when you choose its link; the draft contains no account information or page query parameters.

Website visitor estimate

A random browser identifier in local storage helps estimate distinct visitors across pages using the shared platform header. The server retains only a compact statistical aggregate, not these identifiers. The homepage footer shows the estimate since activation, with typical estimation error around 3%. Clearing storage or using another browser can count again. This counter does not identify people or use fingerprinting. Existing hosting logs and temporary network rate limits are separate.

Public tools and your device

Ritual Assist checklists, Moment Studio card inputs, public team/list tools and break activities generally stay in the current tab. Refresh clears their transient inputs. Downloading or sharing deliberately creates or sends a copy. Timetable Lite saves its weekly setup on your device; Moment Studio saves organization details only when you choose that option. Theme and some interface preferences use browser storage. Device storage and downloaded files remain accessible to people with access to that device.

Remembrance calculations

Ritual Assist sends the entered death date, IST time, Indian PIN code and optional family overrides to this platform’s server to calculate the calendar. It does not save a death record or send those details to Drik Panchang. Request bodies are not logged by the calculator; hosting/network metadata and temporary rate-limit counters may still be processed. PIN coordinates are resolved from a local GeoNames postal snapshot; death details are not sent to a geocoding provider. The returned calendar stays in the tab unless you download or print it. Calculation requires internet and the server connection.

Ritual calendar reminders and preparation packs are generated locally from the returned dates. Pack selections, material checks and responsibility names stay in the current tab and clear on refresh or a new calculation. Downloading/printing creates a copy; importing an ICS file shares its included observance dates with your chosen calendar provider. The platform does not connect your calendar account, synchronize events or send reminder messages.

Accounts and managed workspaces

Account details, password hashes, sessions, app access and preferences are stored by the platform. Chambers stores client/case documents, hearings, tasks and fees. DIGITAL SAMAJ stores submitted family/person details, optional photos, relationships, reviews, consent records and messages. BatchFee Lite stores academy/student/fee records. Tournament Lite stores organizer data and results. These workspaces use the platform database and authorized access checks.

Community field visibility limits application access. Database/machine operators and authorized full-backup administrators can still access stored information. This is not end-to-end encryption. Submit another person’s information or photograph only with appropriate authority; confirm guardian authority for minors. Avoid unnecessary health, contact or exact-address information.

Third-party advertising

Google AdSense may load on eligible free public tools when you are not signed in. If ads are served, Google may use cookies or similar technologies and process browser, device, network and page-visit information to select and measure ads under Google?s own privacy terms. You can manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center. Publisher approval, ad delivery and privacy-message settings depend on Google account configuration; the platform does not guarantee that an ad will appear. Confessions and other public user-submission pages are excluded from this integration.

Public results, sources and external services

Organizer-published tournament links make the permitted results visible to anyone holding the link. News editions are public and may remain archived. Fund comparison links identify public scheme selections. Check what you are sharing before sending a link.

Fund Lens requests public scheme/holdings data from MFapi, Tickertape and Groww; News requests publisher material including Indian Express and BBC. Public reference data and news editions may be cached or stored. Provider permissions are under review; a source citation does not mean an affiliation or licence. Ritual calculations use the platform backend and do not submit entered death details to Drik Panchang.

Cloudflare serves the website and handles network traffic. Hosting and security systems can process request metadata such as IP addresses. Hosting analytics settings may also collect performance metrics. A local-only input statement concerns that tool’s entered data, not all website traffic. External source, payment, email and sharing destinations have their own policies.

The footer language preference (English or Hindi) is saved in this browser when local storage is available. It does not translate private records or submit content to a translation service.

Satire Arena browser voting

Satire Arena saves votes in a separate Cloudflare SQL database, independent of the laptop server. A signed browser cookie identifies this browser for one changeable daily reaction per character. Stored ballots contain a keyed browser identifier, cartoon character, reaction, IST round and update time; they do not contain your platform account, name, phone or raw IP. Hosting request logs and IP-based anti-spam limits are separate. Public scores show totals; your own selections, export and removal use your cookie. Clearing cookies prevents access to older choices and may allow another vote. Ballots remain until removed; no automatic expiry is currently configured. Support contributions never purchase votes.

Retention, removal and requests

Some stored records, drafts, audit histories, original photo blobs and backups remain retained. Removing a community photo from a profile detaches it; retained copies may still exist. No universal self-service erasure or automatic expiry is currently offered. Required legal/financial records and legal holds may limit deletion. Do not assume a profile removal immediately erases every backup.

For access, correction, withdrawal or deletion requests involving a managed workspace, contact its administrator and identify the relevant app and record. Do not send passwords or confidential documents through public channels. A request must be reviewed for identity, authority, retention and other people’s information; this page does not send a request.

Google AdSense

The public homepage loads Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses and browsing information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. AdSense code is not loaded on sign-in screens, private workspaces or tool forms. Ad approval, available inventory and your privacy choices affect whether ads appear. See Google advertising policies and Google ad settings. Advertising preferences and any required regional consent are managed through Google's configured privacy messages; this integration alone does not confirm that those messages are configured.

Payments and sponsorship

Support payments, when enabled, occur in an external UPI/payment service. This platform does not collect payment credentials or independently verify a completed contribution. Contact, support and sponsorship forms prepare local drafts. Opening WhatsApp sends the combined fields to WhatsApp as a prefilled message addressed to our contact team; you press Send to deliver it. The platform does not save these forms or confirm delivery. WhatsApp and the recipient handle sent messages under their own policies. Copying a message creates a separate copy. Submitting an enquiry opens WhatsApp with the combined fields; you review it and press Send there. Reviewed paid placements are labelled. An advertisement preview does not mean an ad network is connected.

Questions and updates

See contact information for the current support status. Material changes to these practices should be reflected in a revised notice. Operator identity, retention schedules and dedicated rights handling remain items for completion; this notice is not a claim of legal compliance.

Public confession stream

Confessions asks only for message text, without a name, email or sign-in. New CAPTCHA-checked submissions are stored in the platform database and shared with everyone using the stream. Older pending submissions remain private until reviewed. Do not include identifying information. The scratch cover is not encryption. Posts become unavailable after seven days; expired entries are pruned on a later successful write. Reports hide messages for further review. Hosting services may retain technical logs, and temporary network rate limits are applied.

The reveal allowance stores card IDs and a remaining count in this tab’s session storage, not message text. Unsent drafts are not saved. Optional ad placements on Confessions are currently disabled. Existing homepage advertising is separate. If optional Confessions ads are enabled later, Google may process device, network and cookie information.

Public feedback and feature requests

Feedback requests and comments are stored in the platform database and published with the label Community member. Do not submit personal details, passwords or confidential business information. A random HttpOnly browser cookie, lasting up to one year, supports one changeable vote per request and browser. Only a hash of that cookie is stored with contributions; the board does not store account names, emails or IP addresses. Clearing cookies or using another browser creates a different voting identity. Hosting providers may separately retain technical logs. Public content remains until moderated; there is no automatic expiry. Administrators can update statuses and hide requests or comments. Reports queue for review. Contact the operator through the contact page about removal.
