# Moment Studio

Moment Studio is one public app for certificates, awards, greetings, invitations, condolences and remembrance cards. The certificate editor opens at `/moment-studio` and `/moment-studio/certificates`. Cards open at `/moment-studio/cards`; use `?occasion=condolence` or `?occasion=remembrance` on that card route for the memorial section. The three sections use the same compact navigation, warm light/dark workspace, details inspector and live preview.

Cards start with a visible occasion and English/Hindi card language, followed by recipient, headline, editable message, sender, additional details and an optional photo. Invitations show event name, guest name, date, time, venue and host. Condolences use sympathy wording; remembrance offers an optional gathering disclosure, with date/time/venue included in the artwork only while that gathering is enabled. On phones the form comes before the preview and download; design choices start collapsed so creation begins with the details. On desktop the inspector scrolls beside the preview. Colour and square/vertical size choices live in More options.

Safe local starter wording follows the selected occasion and language until the user edits it. Changing a design, occasion or language preserves manually entered headline/message, names, additional details and photos. Explicitly applying suggested wording changes only the message and asks before replacing an edited message. Download PNG is the main action, Share card is secondary, and copying message text is an optional action beside the message. Native sharing cancellation preserves the card; unsupported sharing downloads the image for manual attachment.

Wording uses editable English/Hindi starters and manual input.

Cards retain their existing local artwork: Confetti, Elegant, Bloom and Bold for celebrations, and Quiet remembrance, Elegant and Bloom for memorial cards. Exports are 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920 PNG. Preview and export share one renderer; artwork-fit or validation failures disable export until the content fits. Photos stay local, accept PNG/JPEG/WebP up to 5 MB and 24 megapixels, and resize to at most 1600 pixels before centre cropping. Removing or replacing a photo cancels stale loads.

Certificates have four authored A4 landscape designs: Laurel, Nocturne, Summit and Jubilee. Seventeen certificate types and awards retain donation amount wording, organisation branding, signing details and optional local logo/signature images. PDF/print use 300 DPI; PNG offers 300/600 DPI. Up to ten organisation names/logos may be explicitly saved in this browser under the existing organisation storage key. Recipient details and signatures are not saved. Legacy template names map to the new compositions; legacy catalogue preference IDs still map to Moment Studio without rewriting platform data.

All artwork includes the official same-origin Sushant Synapse logo/name through `branding.mjs`. Exports and certificate printing require the brand logo to load. No external AI service, paid tier, checkout, watermark-removal endpoint, external delivery or verification provider is connected. Card and personal certificate content stays in tab memory and clears on refresh. Explicitly saved organisation branding and the shared theme preference remain browser-local.

The retired `/certificates`, `/celebration-studio` and old asset URLs return 404. Current source lives entirely in `apps/moment-studio/frontend`; the old app workspaces retain compatibility tests and historical documentation. No new API, schema, upload or business persistence is introduced. Implementation, test evidence and production release status are recorded separately in `docs/FUNCTIONALITY_LEDGER.md`.
