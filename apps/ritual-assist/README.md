## Panchang remembrance calendar

The result also includes a compact **next ten years** table: ten upcoming Barsi/Samvatsari annual Shraddha candidates and ten upcoming Pitru Paksha candidates, calculated separately from today's IST date. Old death dates retain their first-year history above while the future table starts today. Each series keeps its Hindu tithi/month identity; Gregorian years are never added to a previous result. The first Pitru candidate remains after first Barsi. Rows expose tithi/Aparahna details and conditional/withheld status. Text export includes all events once in chronological order, including the future candidates; printing opens interval details. Unknown/unconfirmed death tithi leaves both future series empty. Ten upcoming occurrences in each series can extend slightly beyond ten Gregorian years, particularly before the first Barsi. This does not add provider certification or guarantee a family-specific ritual rule.

`/ritual-assist/calendar` accepts a past death date, optional IST death time or priest-confirmed tithi, Indian observance place, Amanta/Purnimanta month convention and civil/sunrise day counting. It calculates inclusive Chautha and days 10, 11, 12 (13 optional), regular monthly Masik candidates, Barsi/first annual Shraddha and the first matching Pitru Paksha tithi **after Barsi**. It does not count Gregorian 30-day months or 365-day anniversaries. English/Hindi results include tithi intervals, local Aparahna, conditions, text download and print/PDF. Editing inputs immediately clears old results; details never enter URLs, storage or APIs.

`frontend/panchang.mjs` owns validation, lunar boundaries, sidereal month classification and export. A module worker calculates off the main thread. Vendored Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 calculates geocentric Moon–Sun separation; each 12° is a tithi. Month classification uses the Chitra/Spica-aligned sidereal Sun, **not exact Lahiri/Swiss Ephemeris**. Aparahna is the fourth of five equal sunrise-to-sunset parts; the maximum tithi overlap selects a candidate. No overlap, close overlaps and five-minute boundary guards withhold a definite date. Unknown time produces early day counts and all civil-date tithis, withholding lunar recurrence until a time or confirmed tithi is supplied. Adhik Maas retains regular Masik numbering and labels the extra candidate; death in Adhik Maas and uncertain/kshaya month classification require family confirmation. Purnima/Chaturdashi and exceptional death circumstances require a tradition-specific Pitru Paksha rule. The first Pitru Paksha policy is explicit rather than inferred as a universal obligation.

This is an approximate astronomical planning aid, **not a certified regional Panchang or priest-reviewed service**. Minute-rounded timings matched selected independent Drik Panchang 2026 tithi fixtures within three minutes, and sunrise/sunset within two minutes. That limited validation does not establish exhaustive historical/future agreement. Source links describe the method; no live provider is connected and no ritual obligation is guaranteed. See [third-party provenance](THIRD_PARTY.md). Supported death years are 1950 through the present (maximum 2098), Indian coordinates and IST only.
