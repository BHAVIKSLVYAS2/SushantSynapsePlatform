Why a form may reject an image

Does a smaller KB number mean the image is ready?

No. A form may specify both a minimum and maximum file size, exact pixels, an aspect ratio and a format. A JPEG below the minimum can fail even if it looks clear. This tool checks digital limits and shows mismatches; the receiving authority makes the acceptance decision.

How do I prepare a passport-style photo or signature?

Copy the current dimensions and format from your own application. Choose exact crop for fixed pixels, review the whole face or signature, then resize. There is no universal passport or exam specification. Some forms require live capture, which an edited upload cannot replace.

Can I change formats or remove a background?

JPEG, PNG and WebP output depend on your browser. JPEG makes transparency white; PNG and WebP can retain it. This tool does not remove backgrounds, sharpen missing detail, certify identity or alter signature strokes. Retake an unsuitable source instead.

Are these official presets and file-size guarantees?

Source-linked profiles cover only the named document and date. The SSC CHT 2026 notice is historical, not a current open application or a rule for other SSC exams. Custom size checks use 1 KB = 1024 bytes. Source profiles use conservative byte caps where KB/MB conventions differ. The live form and latest official notice take priority.
